About

AliExpress is a e-commerce platform dedicated to cheap products with a vast selection. It is similar to Amazon, Temu, and Wish in its current advertising and UX approach, generally focusing on fulfilling the needs of consumerism. Each platform contributes to a growing problem of fast fashion, over consumption, and unethical buying.
