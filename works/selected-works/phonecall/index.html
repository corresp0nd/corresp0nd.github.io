Phonecall

Phonecall is an advertising campaign combining Barbie and Scream.

fall 2023

About

The project was to combine two already existing movies and then create an advertising campaign for the result. I chose Barbie and Scream to create Phonecall, a pink and campy slasher movie. Most of the posters are photobashed and edited together, though there are some minor drawn elements. The third poster, with the phone on the ground, is an illustration. Each poster was done in Adobe Photoshop.
