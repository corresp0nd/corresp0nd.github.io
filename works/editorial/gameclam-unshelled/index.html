GameClam Unshelled

A magazine for the fake game console, GameClam.

fall 2024

About

This was a semester long project creating a magazine publisher, one of their issues, and a small advertising campaign for them. The final deliverables were a 12 page booklet, a brand guidelines presentation, a desktop and mobile mockup of their website, and a potential "giveaway" item, which I made a 3D model of a GameClam shaped bag.
Most of the inspiration for this project came from early 2000s gaming magazines, so most of the aesthetics and layouts mirror the era.
